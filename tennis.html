Table Tennis Competition

Tagline

In the table tennis competition, each school may register a maximum of 5 players, consisting of 2 doubles pairs and 1 singles player. A total of 12 teams will compete in the tournament and will be divided into four pools, with 3 teams in each pool. Every team in each pool will compete against one another in the preliminary round, and the winner of each pool will advance to the semifinals. The semifinal matches will follow a knockout system, with the winners advancing to the finals and the losing teams competing for third place.

Each match will use a best-of-5 system, with each game played to 11 points and requiring a 2-point difference to win. Players will also be given designated break and timeout opportunities during matches. All participants are expected to follow the tournament rules, arrive on time, wear appropriate sports attire, and demonstrate good sportsmanship throughout the competition. Science
